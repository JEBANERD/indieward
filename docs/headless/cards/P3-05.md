# P3-05: IndieWard name + fork policy (needs Jasper)

MIT lets anyone fork IndieWard and remove its safety gates (store verification, no-send, weak-evidence refusal). A licence change would end "open source". The cleaner lever is the name: a fork can exist, but can't call itself IndieWard.

Jasper: search and consider registering "IndieWard" at the USPTO (Class 9 software, maybe Class 42), then have the worker add TRADEMARKS.md: forks are welcome under MIT, but builds that remove the verification gate, the no-send rule or the evidence labels may not use the IndieWard name or logo. Not legal advice; a short consult is worth it before filing.
