**IndieWard is out: free anti-clone toolkit for indie games** 🛡️

**What it can't do:** stop anyone copying or decompiling your game. Nothing can, AI tools included.

**What it does**, locally on your machine, never sending anything for you:
• Signed, timestamped proof of your art, audio, code, text and builds
• Invisible watermarks for images, audio and video, plus inert trap markers in builds
• Clone checks with Strong / Supporting / Weak scores per category ("don't file" when only mechanics match)
• Takedown drafts for Steam, itch.io and Roblox that you review and send yourself

**Install:** `pipx install "indieward[mcp]"` then `indieward doctor`
**Guide:** https://github.com/JEBANERD/indieward/blob/main/docs/creator-guide.md
