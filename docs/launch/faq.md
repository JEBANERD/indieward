# IndieWard FAQ

**Can IndieWard stop people or AI from copying my game?**
No. A shipped game runs on the player's machine, so it can be decompiled, and footage can be copied. IndieWard makes proof cheap and filing fast. It doesn't block anything.

**Can it tell whether a clone was made with AI?**
No. A score measures similarity to your registered work. Nobody can see another party's prompts or logs, and every report says so.

**Does it send takedowns for me?**
No. It drafts the notice and a checklist for Steam, itch.io or Roblox. You review it and send it from your own account. IndieWard never sends email or submits forms.

**What are trap markers? Will they affect my game?**
Made-up strings, constants, a dead function and a tiny hidden image, recorded privately in your vault. They're never shown, they're read once at startup, and they don't touch gameplay, performance, the network or player data. Finding one in a suspect build is strong evidence it was ripped from yours.

**Why do I have to verify my store page?**
So nobody else can register your game in their name and file against you. Registering, trapping, checking and packaging all need a verified store link.

**Is my data uploaded anywhere?**
No. Your vault stays in your project folder. The only network calls are the ones you run, such as timestamping or reading a store page.
